# ADR-0005: Apache-2.0 plus trademark policy

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-14

## Context

The extension is open source, owned by DuckDiveLab Pte. Ltd., and used in its own warehouse.
"ParcelHaste" is a DuckDiveLab trademark; forks must not ship under that name.

## Decision

- License: Apache-2.0, copyright 2026 DuckDiveLab Pte. Ltd. `NOTICE` carries the attribution.
- `TRADEMARKS.md`: the name and logo are not licensed; forks rename.
- Contributions are accepted under the same license; AI-assisted contributions are welcome and the
  human author is responsible for them (CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Consequences

- Store listings are published only by DuckDiveLab.
- No contributor license agreement; the Apache-2.0 section 5 grant applies.
